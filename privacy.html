Summary

King Snake does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information, from anyone, including children. The game makes no network requests of any kind while you play - there is no ad SDK, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind built into the app.

What is stored, and where

Your best score, rank, play stats, and settings (nickname, chosen skin, control scheme, sound on/off, and language) are saved only in your device's local browser storage (localStorage), directly on your device. This data never leaves your device - it is not sent to us, to any server, or to any third party.

  • Nothing is uploaded, synced, or backed up to the cloud.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing site data deletes this data along with it.
  • There is no account or login of any kind, so there is nothing to link this data to your identity.

Children's privacy

King Snake does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your region). Since the game makes no network requests and requires no account, no data about any player - adult or child - is ever collected or transmitted.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the "Last updated" date above will be updated accordingly.
