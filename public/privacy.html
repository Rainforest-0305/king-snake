Summary

King Snake does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information, from anyone, including children. Gameplay itself makes no network requests. Ads are shown in two ways: a full-screen ad appears between runs (at most every other run, and no more than three times a day), and there are optional rewarded ads that only play when you tap a button to ask for one. Those ads use the Apps in Toss in-app ad SDK; Toss performs the processing needed to serve them, and we neither collect nor pass on anything that identifies you. No analytics and no tracking of any kind are built into the app.

What is stored, and where

Everything below is saved only in your device's local browser storage (localStorage), directly on your device. It never leaves your device - it is not sent to us, to any server, or to any third party. The items recorded on your device are all of the following, and nothing else:

ItemWhat it holds
king-snake.saveBest score, best rank, runs played, play time, kills, nickname, chosen skin, control scheme, sound on/off, language
king-snake.ads.v1Full-screen ad pacing - runs played, when an ad was last shown, how many were shown today
king-snake.reward.v1Rewarded-ad ledger - how many rewards of each kind were granted today, and the date
king-snake.adseq.v1The last 8 ad events, for diagnosing playback problems - ad type, event name, timestamp
king-snake.adwatch.v1How many ads you watched toward unlocking skins
king-snake.unlock.v1Which skins you have unlocked
king-snake.carry.v1The share of this run's growth carried into your next run's starting length, and whether it was doubled
king-snake.push.v1Your notification choice - whether you agreed or declined, and when. If you never open the notification prompt, this item is never created
king-snake.xpromo.v1The date the "other app" recommendation card was last shown, so it appears at most once a day
king-snake.promo.v1: + promotion codeYour progress in one promotion - one item per promotion that is running. Nothing is stored for promotions you have not taken part in

Inside the Toss app, two of the items above - king-snake.ads.v1 and king-snake.push.v1 - are also kept in the app's own storage so they survive a reinstall. Those are copies of the same values, not additional items.

  • Nothing is uploaded, synced, or backed up to the cloud.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing site data deletes this data along with it.
  • There is no account or login of any kind, so there is nothing to link this data to your identity.

Notifications

Notifications are off unless you turn them on. If you tap "Turn on" in Settings, the Toss app asks you to agree; we record only whether you agreed or declined and when (king-snake.push.v1). We do not receive or store any device token or push address - the Toss app handles delivery. You can turn notifications off at any time in the Toss app's notification settings.

Children's privacy

King Snake does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your region). The game requires no account and stores nothing that identifies a player, so no data about any player - adult or child - is ever collected or transmitted. The only network activity is the ad SDK fetching an ad.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the "Last updated" date above will be updated accordingly.

요약

뱀 키우기는 아동을 포함한 그 누구로부터도 개인정보를 수집·전송·공유하지 않습니다. 게임 플레이 자체로는 어떤 네트워크 요청도 발생하지 않습니다. 광고는 두 가지 방식으로 표시됩니다. 판과 판 사이에 전면광고가 표시되며 (최대 두 판마다 한 번, 하루 3회까지), 그와 별도로 버튼을 눌러 직접 요청했을 때만 재생되는 보상형 광고가 있습니다. 두 경우 모두 앱스인토스 인앱광고를 사용합니다. 광고 노출을 위해 필요한 처리는 토스가 수행하며, 저희는 이용자를 식별하는 정보를 수집하거나 전달하지 않습니다. 애널리틱스·추적 기능은 포함되어 있지 않습니다.

알림

알림은 사용자가 켜기 전에는 꺼져 있습니다. 설정에서 「받기」를 누르면 토스 앱이 동의를 묻고, 저희는 동의했는지 거부했는지와 그 시각만 기록합니다(king-snake.push.v1). 기기 토큰이나 푸시 주소는 저희가 받지도, 저장하지도 않습니다 — 발송은 토스 앱이 처리합니다. 알림 끄기는 언제든 토스 앱의 알림 설정에서 할 수 있습니다.
